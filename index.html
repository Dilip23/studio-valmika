We design spaces that feel connected to where they belong.

Studio Valmika is an architecture and design studio based in Visakhapatnam, creating nature-inspired residences, luxury villas, boutique resorts, modern workplaces and thoughtfully designed interiors.

Our work brings together natural materials, earthy textures, sustainable thinking and contemporary architecture to create spaces that feel timeless rather than transient.

Selected projects

Homes, retreats and interiors, each drawn from its own ground.

Form follows place.

We shape buildings around climate, landscape, materials, light and the way people actually inhabit space, so that each one looks as if it was always going to be there.

  • Luxury villas Private homes with generous gardens
  • Residential homes Family houses shaped by climate
  • Boutique resorts Small hospitality, quietly sited
  • Modern offices Calm workplaces with daylight and air

Interiors with warmth, texture and restraint.

Natural wood, handmade ceramics, linen, stone and warm light. Rooms that feel lived in, calm and quietly sure of themselves.

A studio from the coast, inspired by the earth.

Studio Valmika is based in Visakhapatnam, between the Bay of Bengal and the Eastern Ghats. We work across architecture, interiors and craft, and the three keep teaching each other.

A drawing board and a potter's wheel share the same room. That keeps us close to materials, to experiments, and to the idea that a building and a bowl should both be made with care.
